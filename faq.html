Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about who we are, what we do, and how our independent service assistance platform works.

About Our Platform

Are you an official cable or internet service provider?

No. Safinternet is an independent third-party service assistance platform. We are not an Internet Service Provider (ISP) and we do not own, operate, or represent any cable or internet network. Our role is entirely limited to providing general guidance and informational support.

Are you affiliated with any telecom or cable company?

No. Safinternet is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or sponsored by any internet, cable, telecom, or streaming service provider. Any references to providers on our platform are for informational purposes only.

When was Safinternet founded?

Safinternet is a startup launched in 2026. We are a newly established independent service assistance platform focused on helping users navigate internet and cable service options with clarity and confidence.

Our Services

Do you sell internet, cable, or streaming plans?

No. We do not sell any internet, cable, or streaming service plans. We provide general guidance and informational assistance only. To subscribe to a service, you would need to contact a provider directly.

What kind of assistance do you provide?

We help users understand internet and broadband service options, get practical guidance on common connectivity concerns, learn how to navigate service provider processes, and receive general informational support related to cable TV services.

Which providers do you support or reference?

We may reference multiple providers such as Kinetic™, HughesNet®, AT&T®, Frontier®, Optimum®, EarthLink®, Viasat®, and DirecTV® for general informational purposes only. We are not affiliated with, partnered with, or endorsed by any of these companies. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Fees & Billing

Do you charge for your services?

Yes. Safinternet charges a separate service assistance fee for the independent guidance we provide. This fee is entirely separate from any provider subscription charges.

Will I still be billed by my service provider?

Yes. If you independently choose to subscribe to an internet, cable, or streaming service provider, their billing is entirely separate from and independent of Safinternet's service assistance fees. Provider billing remains unchanged and is governed solely by the provider's terms.

Are your services refundable?

Refund requests may be submitted within 7 to 15 days of your service assistance purchase, depending on the type of assistance provided and whether the service was fully delivered. Please review our Refund Policy page for full eligibility details. Refunds are not automatically guaranteed and are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Privacy & Data

Do you need my account passwords or sensitive information?

Absolutely not. Safinternet never requests provider passwords, account login credentials, or any sensitive account information. We only ask for general information (such as your location or service concerns) to provide relevant guidance.

How do you handle my personal data?

We collect only the information necessary to provide our assistance services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your information.
